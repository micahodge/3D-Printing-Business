# 3D-Printing-Business

<h2>Description</h2>
Started and run a 3D-printing business at home. This project's purpose was so I could fund personal engineering projects the NASA HERC Rover engineering team at my school. I started the business in January 2026, directly after finishing my internship at NASA LaRC. I started selling 3D-printed socket organizers on marketplaces like Etsy and eBay. I started out using a Bambu Lab P2S printer. Currently, I now have 3 P2S printers due to the scale of the business. 
<br />

<h2>Tool/Softwares</h2>

- <b>Bambu Lab P2S 3D-Printer (3x)</b> 
- <b>Bambu Studio</b>
- <b>SolidWorks</b>
